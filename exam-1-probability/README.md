# Exam 1 Probability

This repository contains the materials for Exam 1 in Probability.

## Contents

1. Probability

    - **Sample Space** - Chance outcomes, numerical and categorical data, observation, experiment, sample space, tree diagrams, and sampling plan.
    - **Events** - Events, null set, subsets, complement of an event, intersection and union of an event, mutually exclusive/disjoint events, and venn diagrams.
    - **Counting Sample Points** - Fundamental rule of counting, generalized multiplication rule, permutations, circular permutations, set partitions, and combinations.
    - **Probability of an Event** - Likelihood of an event, probability of an event A, and relative frequency definition of probability.
    - **Additive Rules** - additive rule of union of two events, additive rule of union of three events, and general additive rule.
    - **Conditional Probability** - Conditional probability, independent events, and multiplication rule.
    - **Bayes Rule** - Total probability theorem, Bayes rule, and applications of Bayes rule.

2. Random Variables and Distributions

    - **Intro to Random Variables** - Definition of random variables, discrete and continuous random variables, discrete and continuous sample space, definition of bernoulli random variable.
    - **Discrete Probability Distributions** - Probability mass function, cumulative distribution function for discrete random variables, and probability histograms.
    - **Continuous Probability Distributions** - Probability density function, cumulative distribution function for continuous random variables.
